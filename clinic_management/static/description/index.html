Run The Clinic On Standard Odoo 19

Clinic & Hospital Management covers the front desk, the doctor, the ward, the pharmacy and the lab. Patients, appointments, admissions, prescriptions and test requests stay linked, and invoices post through Odoo Accounting on the same products and stock your inventory already uses.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Live Clinic Dashboard

Patients, appointments, admissions, beds, revenue and unpaid invoices update together, with shortcuts for the work reception does most.

OPD From Book To Consult

Book the doctor and slot, work today's queue, then record blood pressure, diagnosis and follow-up on the consultation.

IPD, Rooms And Discharge

Admit to a free bed, watch occupancy on the bed board, and close the stay with a printable discharge summary.

Pharmacy, Lab And Billing

Prescriptions and lab requests use Odoo products and post as standard customer invoices through Accounting.

Clinic App On The Odoo Home

After installation, Clinic appears on the Odoo home screen beside Discuss, Invoicing, Inventory and the other apps your clinic already uses.

Live Clinic Dashboard

Open the day with patients, appointments, admissions, free and occupied beds, revenue and pending payments. Register a patient, book a visit, admit someone or raise a lab request from the same screen.

Patient Medical Record

Keep gender, date of birth, blood group, contact, emergency contact and existing diseases on one form, with history, visits, appointments, admissions, prescriptions and lab tests underneath.

Book An Appointment

Choose the patient, doctor, department, date, duration and consultation fee, then note the symptoms before the visit is confirmed.

Today's Appointments

Reception sees today's confirmed and draft visits as cards, with the doctor, time, visit type and fee, and can start the consultation from the card.

All Appointments

The full appointment list shows reference, patient, doctor, department, type, fee and state, from draft and confirmed through checked in, no show and completed.

OPD Consultations

Each outpatient visit records check-in time, blood pressure, diagnosis and follow-up, and moves from checked in to completed.

Inpatient Admissions

Admitted patients are listed with the attending doctor, department, admission type, bed, length of stay and current state.

Discharge Summary

A discharge moves from draft to doctor approved and then discharged. The summary holds the final diagnosis and treatment, and prints as a discharge report.

Bed Board

Beds are grouped by room. Available beds can take a new admission, occupied beds open the current admission, and beds under maintenance stay blocked.

Prescription

A confirmed prescription is tied to the patient, doctor and OPD visit. Each line shows dosage, frequency, duration, quantity, stock and price before the medicines are dispensed.

Medicine On Inventory

Medicines are standard Odoo products. Mark a product as a medicine, set the category, form and strength, and track quantity on hand.

Laboratory Request

A lab request follows the sample from requested to collected to completed. Results sit next to the normal range, with an abnormal flag, remarks and price, and can be printed or invoiced.

Lab Test Catalog

Maintain every test with its code, sample type, normal range, unit and price, across biochemistry, hematology, microbiology and radiology.

Patient Invoice

Dispensed medicines and clinic services post as a normal Odoo customer invoice, with tax, total and the amount still due.

Outstanding Payments

Unpaid invoices are grouped by patient, so billing can see who still owes, how long the invoice has been due, and the clinic total.

Doctors

Doctors are grouped by department, with working hours, consultation fee and how many appointments they have today.

Staff Members

Nurses, pharmacists, laboratory technicians, receptionists and administrative staff are organised by role and department.

Clinic Reporting

Reporting covers patients, appointments, doctor performance, admissions, discharges, bed occupancy, revenue, outstanding payments, pharmacy and laboratory, in charts, pivots and lists.

Departments

Each department has a code, a head of department and the doctors who work in it.

Specializations

Specializations such as cardiology, dermatology, ENT, gynecology and pediatrics are maintained once and used on doctor profiles.

Diseases And Conditions

Conditions such as asthma, diabetes and hypertension are named and colour-tagged so they show clearly on the patient record.

Room Categories

Set a default daily rate for general ward, ICU, private and semi-private rooms.

Rooms And Beds

A room stores its floor, category and beds, and each bed has a type, daily charge and available, occupied or maintenance state.

Filter The Bed Board

Filter the same bed board by room category and bed state before admitting a patient into a free bed.

Medicine Categories

Group the pharmacy into analgesics, antibiotics, antidiabetics, antihypertensives, vitamins and any other category you need.

Lab Test Categories

Group the test catalog into biochemistry, hematology, microbiology and radiology.
